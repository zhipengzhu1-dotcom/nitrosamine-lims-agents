# China NMPA requirements beyond the FDA baseline

Research for [#26](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/26), part of map [#1](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/1). Sources fetched 2026-09-29. All lab data, products and batches in this project are fictional. The regulatory text is real and is quoted from the sources at the end.

The baselines are [docs/research/part11.md](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/blob/research/part11/docs/research/part11.md) (#2) and [docs/research/nitrosamines.md](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/blob/research/nitrosamines/docs/research/nitrosamines.md) (#4). This file lists only what China adds, tightens or does differently. Where China matches or asks for less than the baseline, this file says nothing more about it.

## Short answer

**Computerized systems and records.** If the LIMS is built to the Part 11 baseline (with its draft Annex 11 superset), it already covers most of China. It needs eight additions:

1. **More audit events, each with a reason.** The trail must record "操作者、操作时间、操作过程、操作原因" (operator, time, action, reason) for system operations. It must cover the "产生、修改、删除、再处理、重新命名、转移" (creation, modification, deletion, reprocessing, **renaming, transfer**) of data, and changes to settings, configuration, parameters and **timestamps** [RD Art. 22(4)].
2. **Business users are never administrators at any layer.** A process owner may not hold administrator rights to the "操作系统、应用程序、数据库" (operating system, application or database) [RD Art. 22(1)]. The baseline separates roles inside the app; China extends the rule to the server and the database.
3. **Changing critical data needs approval, not only a reason.** "每次修改已输入的关键数据均应当经过批准，并应当记录更改数据的理由" (every change to entered critical data must be approved, and the reason recorded) [CS Art. 16].
4. **A log of backups and deletions, and validated backup and restore.** "备份与恢复流程必须经过验证，数据的备份与删除应有相应记录" (backup and restore procedures must be validated; backups and deletions must be recorded) [RD Art. 21(3)]. Backups go to "另一个单独的、安全的地点" (a separate, secure location) [CS Art. 19(3)].
5. **A declared primary form per record type.** Where electronic and paper records both exist, a document must say which one is the "主数据" (primary data) [CS Art. 18] or the "基准" (reference form) [RD Art. 6]. The baseline treats this as an FDA recommendation; in China it is a "应当" (shall).
6. **More fields on test records.** These include "必要的环境温湿度" (ambient temperature and humidity where needed), reagent and reference-standard lot and source, instrument **model and number**, and a separate **"检验、计算复核人员"** (test and calculation checker) signature with date [GMP Art. 223(6)].
7. **A default retention period.** Batch test records are kept "至少保存至药品有效期后一年" (at least until one year after the product's expiry). Specifications, SOPs, stability, validation and change records are kept "长期" (long-term) [GMP Art. 162].
8. **E-signatures under the PRC Electronic Signature Law.** No. 74 points to it directly [RD Art. 22(3)]. A "可靠的电子签名" (reliable electronic signature) has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature **or a seal** [ESL Art. 13–14]. The baseline design (signing data only the signer controls, plus a content hash that shows later edits) meets its four conditions. The law does **not** require a third-party certificate authority (CA). It also sets no rule for showing name, date and meaning, so Part 11 stays the stricter rule there.

**Nitrosamines.** CDE's only guidance is still the **2020 trial version**. It shares FDA's TD50 formula (50 kg body weight, 1-in-100,000 risk), its `limit = AI ÷ maximum daily dose` rule and **NDMA at 96 ng/day** [CDE-N §三(二), §四]. It has none of FDA's later machinery: no CPCA, no 10 %-of-AI trigger for a specification, no rule for several nitrosamines, and no less-than-lifetime adjustment. Its worked example puts **NDIPA and NEIPA at 26.5 ng/day**, while FDA now uses 1500 and 400 ng/day. Where no TD50 exists, CDE says to take the **smallest** limit from the available approaches. A lab's own method must be shown "等效于或者更优于同品种官方公布的方法" (equivalent to or better than the official published method for the same product) [CDE-N §三(三)]. The Chinese Pharmacopoeia (ChP) 2025 has **no dedicated nitrosamine test chapter**. Its genotoxic-impurity guidance 9306 is recommended rather than mandatory, and defers to the latest ICH text. ChP rounding follows **GB/T 8170-2008** (round half to even, not half up) [ChP-I1].

**What this means for the LIMS.** Make the jurisdiction a property of the product specification, and key the AI table, the decision rules and the rounding mode on it. Do not build one global FDA rule set.

## Source access notes

- **nmpa.gov.cn HTML pages** (the announcement pages for No. 54/2015 and No. 74/2020) returned **HTTP 412**, a JavaScript bot challenge, to WebFetch, curl and a headless browser. The **attachment files on the same host downloaded fine**, so the appendix, the No. 74 requirements, the CDE guidance and the ChP 2025 table of contents were all read in the original from nmpa.gov.cn. Announcement numbers and dates come from mirrors run by government bodies, as noted in Sources.
- **cde.org.cn** dynamic guidance listings returned the same kind of JavaScript challenge. So this research **could not confirm from CDE directly** that no nitrosamine guidance later than 2020 exists. Web searches found none. See open question 6.
- **chp.org.cn** refused connections or timed out. So the text of ChP 9306 and of the 2025 General Notices (凡例) was **not read**. The ChP statements here come from the Chinese Pharmacopoeia Commission's own articles on the 2025 edition, published by 中国食品药品网 (cnpharm.com, the China Pharmaceutical News site that NMPA oversees) and signed "国家药典委员会供稿" (contributed by the ChP Commission) [ChP-I1, ChP-I2].
- **GB/T 8170-2008**: the official page (openstd.samr.gov.cn) confirms the title, status and dates, but its full text would not open. The round-half-to-even rule comes from search-result summaries of the standard, not from the standard itself.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Chinese localization of records.** None of the fetched sources requires records or the UI to be in Chinese. GMP asks only that SOPs use "准确、易懂的语言" (accurate, understandable language) [GMP Art. 10(4)], and the ESL and No. 74 say nothing about language. Language rules for registration dossiers were not checked. Proposed default: store all text as Unicode so Chinese product names (for example 缬沙坦), Chinese personal names in signature blocks and Chinese spec text display and print correctly. Also allow a bilingual report template. Leave full UI translation out of scope unless the lab will have Chinese-speaking Analysts or Customers. Does the owner agree?
2. **Is China a real jurisdiction for this demo, or a comparison?** If it is real, the specification needs a `jurisdiction` field (FDA, NMPA, or both). The AI table, the 10 % flag, the LTL factor, the multi-nitrosamine rule and the rounding mode all depend on it. A dual-market product with NDIPA would have to meet the stricter China limit (26.5 against FDA's 1500 ng/day).
3. **Admin separation at the server and database level** [RD Art. 22(1)]. On a single low-cost VPS, who holds root and database-owner access, and are they kept out of Analyst, Reviewer and QA roles? For a one-person demo this may have to be a documented exception.
4. **CA-backed signatures.** The ESL does not require a licensed CA [ESL Art. 16], so password + TOTP with hash binding is lawful. Should the demo also offer PKI or CA-certificate signing for Chinese Customers who expect it? The recommendation is no, unless a Customer asks.
5. **AIs that China does not publish** (NDEA, NDBA, NDPA, NMPA, NMBA, NDSRIs). Should a China spec take the value from the Customer with a justification, or copy the FDA or EMA value as the "most conservative approach" [CDE-N §三(二)2]?
6. **Newer CDE nitrosamine guidance.** cde.org.cn's guidance database could not be queried (bot challenge). Someone with browser access should check it for any revision after the 2020 trial version, and check chp.org.cn for the final ChP 9306 text and General Notices, before this is treated as current.
7. **Approval workflow for changing critical data** [CS Art. 16]. Which fields count as "关键数据" (critical data)? Proposed: result values, sample weights, dilution volumes, standard concentrations, MDD and AI on the spec, and instrument assignment.
8. **Report seals.** The ESL treats a reliable e-signature as equal to a "盖章" (seal) [ESL Art. 14]. Chinese labs often stamp reports with an inspection seal, and accreditation marks (CMA/CNAS) have their own rules. Neither was researched here. Does the report template need a seal or accreditation-mark slot? This belongs with the ISO/IEC 17025 ticket.
