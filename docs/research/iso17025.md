# ISO/IEC 17025:2017 clauses mapped to LIMS modules

Research for [#3](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/3), part of map [#1](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/1). Researched 2026-09-29. All lab data in this project is fictional.

## Short answer

The LIMS touches all 13 clauses in the ticket, and each one comes down to something the software has to keep. That is a record, a gate that blocks a step, or both.

- **Personnel (6.2).** A person must be *authorised*, not just trained, for each activity: running a method, reviewing results, making statements of conformity, and releasing reports. Their competence must also be monitored over time.
- **Equipment (6.4).** "Equipment" includes software, reference materials, reagents and consumables, not just instruments. Each item needs a record with eight fixed fields (a–h), including the software and firmware version.
- **Technical records (7.5).** Each result must be tied to who did the work, who checked it, and when. Any change to a record must keep the original value along with the date, what changed and who changed it. That is the same audit trail Part 11 needs.
- **Nonconforming work (7.10).** This is what this project calls a **Deviation**. Beyond "block the equipment", the Deviation must also record:
  - an assessment of the effect on results already issued;
  - whether reports were held back or recalled, and whether the Customer was told;
  - who authorised the work to restart.
- **Data control (7.11).** The LIMS itself must be validated before use and after every change. It must also log its own system failures, and the cloud host must meet the standard's requirements (7.11.4).
- **Reports (7.8).** A released report must carry 16 fixed items (a–p). Changes after issue must go out as an identified "Amendment to Report" or as a new report that references the one it replaces.

**Source caveat.** Every requirement summary below rests on **secondary sources**: accreditation-body documents that paraphrase the standard. ISO's own pages could not be retrieved. Both the catalogue page (<https://www.iso.org/standard/66912.html>) and the Online Browsing Platform preview returned HTTP 403 or a Cloudflare bot check, to WebFetch, to curl and to a headless browser, on 2026-09-29. No claim here quotes ISO text. The closest stand-in is PJLA's assessor checklist [PJLA], which follows the standard clause by clause, including the lettered sub-items.

### Other clauses the LIMS touches (not in the ticket's list)

- **4.2 Confidentiality.** The lab is responsible for managing information obtained or created during its activities, and personnel keep it confidential [PJLA 4.2.1, 4.2.4]. The portal must only ever show a Customer their own requests and reports.
- **6.6 Externally provided products and services.** Keep a procedure and records for defining requirements, evaluating, selecting and monitoring providers, and verifying that products meet requirements before use [PJLA 6.6.2]. This covers approved suppliers for CRMs and reagents in Inventory, and approved calibration providers.
- **7.2 Methods.** Use the latest valid method version, and verify the lab can perform a method before introducing it [PJLA 7.2.1.3, 7.2.1.5]. The document vault holds methods; results cite a method version.
- **7.9 Complaints.** Track and record complaints and the actions taken to resolve them. Outcomes are reviewed by someone not involved in the original work [PJLA 7.9.3, 7.9.6]. The portal is a natural intake point; not in scope yet.

## Design implications for the open tickets

1. **Training records gate more than assignment** ([#19](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/19)). 6.2.6 requires authorisation for reviewing results and for authorising or releasing reports, as well as for running methods. Model a single `Authorisation(person, activity, scope, from, until)` record that is checked at assignment, at review sign-off and at QA release.
2. **Deviation must cover the full 7.10 lifecycle** ([#16](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/16)). "Blocks the equipment until closed" covers 7.10.1 b) for equipment only. The Deviation also needs an impact assessment on earlier results (c), an acceptability decision (d), customer notification and recall linked to report amendment (e), and a named resumption authoriser (f). Records of b–f are mandatory (7.10.2). The glossary's "Deviation" is this project's name for ISO "nonconforming work"; `CONTEXT.md` already tells readers to avoid "nonconformance".
3. **Inventory items are equipment** ([#15](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/15), [#17](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/17)). Under 6.4.1, reference materials, reagents, consumables and software fall under the equipment clause. They need status and period of validity (6.4.8, 6.4.13 f), and CRM-to-working-standard lineage (6.5, [ANAB §3.3]).
4. **Batch QC is a release gate** ([#20](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/20)). 7.7.3 requires action that prevents incorrect results being reported, so QC failure must block review and release, not just warn.
5. **The report is a versioned, immutable artifact** (Report/CoA, not yet specified). The 7.8.2.1 a–p fields, the 7.8.8 amendment rules and NATA's electronic-report controls define the CoA template and the portal download.
6. **7.11 applies to the LIMS itself** ([#6](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/6), [#14](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/14)). The system needs a change-control log, a system-incident log (7.11.3 e) and a check on PDF/TXT extraction (7.11.6). The cloud host counts as an external provider (7.11.4).
7. **Document vault holds external documents too** ([#18](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/18)). 8.3.1 covers manufacturer's instructions and other external documents, not just in-house SOPs.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Which accreditation body does the fictional lab answer to?** The standard leaves retention and PT frequency open. ANAB (US) requires PT every calendar year and keeping PT records ≥ 4 years [ANAB §1.1.5, §1.3.1]. NATA requires PT every two years and retention of all records ≥ 4 years [NATA-SAD 7.7.2, 8.1.1]. The answer sets the retention floor in 8.4 and the PT schedule in 7.7.2. The Part 11 retention decision ([#2](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/2)) may override either.
2. **Will reports carry pass/fail statements against FDA nitrosamine limits?** If so, 7.1.3 and 7.8.6 require an agreed decision rule at request review, and 7.8.3 may require measurement uncertainty on the report. This depends on [#4](https://github.com/zhipengzhu1-dotcom/09-28-2026-LIMS/issues/4).
3. **7.11.2 validation versus the "no IQ/OQ/PQ" out-of-scope line on #1.** The standard requires the LIMS to be validated before use and after each change. The map puts validation deliverables out of scope. Should the demo still keep a lightweight change-control log with automated test evidence per release, so the mechanism exists without claiming validation? Without it, the demo cannot credibly claim 7.11.2 conformity.
4. **How often is competence re-checked (6.2.5 f)?** Does an authorisation expire, for example after 12 months or after N months without running the method (NATA's "infrequent tests" point)?
5. **Is a LIMS system failure (7.11.3 e) a kind of Deviation, or its own log?**
