## Console errors

None seen. The capture was validated first, by logging `console.error('PROBE')`, `console.warn` and an uncaught error and confirming `agent-browser console` and `agent-browser errors` reported them. The buffer persists across navigations (checked with a second probe). It was read after the deep links, the upload flow, both signing flows, the lockout, the Checks flows, the assign dialog (choose, Cancel, Escape), every queue chip, sort header, search and dropdown option, the lock and switch cycles and an iframe load, and every read was empty.

The one non-200 request was `GET /favicon.ico` 404, because the page declares no icon. The capture reported nothing for it, but a regular Chrome DevTools console normally prints a missing favicon as a line, so an owner on a plain static server may see one.

## Item 20. Broken, misleading or confusing

### Serious

1. **Back, Forward or an edited hash changes the signed-in person silently and removes the lock.** From `#queue` as Hannah, click Test workspace and Sign in as Priya (`#test`). Back then shows Hannah's Lab queue with no dialog or notice (`06-back-button-silent-user-change.png`). With Priya's PC locked, Back removes the lock and shows Hannah Kowalski signed in with no credentials (`07-lock-back-button-bypass.png`, and the same by setting `location.hash` in `07-lock-hash-bypass-attempt.png`). Reading `app.js` afterwards shows why. `hashchange` runs `enterRoute()`, which sets the signed-in person to the route's persona and clears every open dialog. This defeats the shared-PC rules for lock and takeover.
2. **The `#sign` route writes the flag comment and ticks the checklist for the user.** From a fresh `#imported` with nothing handled, setting the hash to `#sign` opens the signature prompt with the flag shown as "Accepted with a comment: 'Identity confirmed: retention time 2.41 min on all four injections and S/N 64; the qualifier ratio is low on this one injection only.'" and all three checklist boxes ticked (`20-sign-route-skips-flag-gate.png`). The flag and checklist gate from item 10 can be skipped, and the record to be signed carries words the signer never wrote.
3. **Switch user carries the previous person's unsaved work and leaves the new person on the same screen.** Priya typed a flag comment and ticked one checklist box, then Lock, Switch user, Hannah Kowalski. The comment text and the tick were still there, Hannah stayed on Priya's Test workspace, and Sign Performed was offered to her (`20-switch-user-carries-unsaved-work.png`, `20-manager-on-analyst-workspace.png`). The dialog says "Anything they had not saved is not carried over". Typed readings on the Checks screen are cleared on a switch (Kenji's 21.5 vanished), so only the workspace state leaks. The same switch from the lock screen leaves a Lab Manager on the Analyst's Today's Checks screen.

### Moderate

4. **The Lab queue ignores the consequences the Checks screen announces.** After BAL-04 failed ("DEV-26-0094 opened (Equipment, Major); BAL-04 Suspended; Holds placed on 14 Tests."), the queue as Hannah still says "38 with Holds" and searching `DEV-26-0094` gives "No open Tests match these filters" (`20-queue-ignores-bal04-holds.png`). Assigning and signing do update the queue.
5. **The Checks form and the keypad fight for space.** At 1366x768 the keypad is hidden by default and tapping a field does not open it (Show keypad, 112x40, must be tapped). With the keypad open on FRZ-01 and an out-of-limit reading, at most two of the five boxes are above the pinned keypad at any scroll position (`20-1366-frz01-keypad-overlap.png`, `20-1366-frz01-scrolled.png`, `20-1366-checks-keypad-shown.png`, `20-1366-checks.png`). At 1920x1080 the Maximum since reset box is cut off and its required confirm box (y 718 to 774) sits behind the keypad (y 644 to 846), while Save reading stays disabled with no visible hint (`20-1920-frz01-max-confirm-behind-keypad.png`). A tap at the hidden box's position lands on a key. Reaching it needs a scroll.
6. **Some secondary text fails WCAG AA.** Measured in the page, the muted text colour `#66718A` on the page background `#E8ECF2` is 4.12:1 for 13 to 14 px text ("for 1 d 1 h", "in 6 d", "due 8 Oct 2026", section sub-titles), and 3.84:1 on the selected Checks row `#DBE4FA` ("Due", "Audited, not signed", "No value yet"). Unselected rows on white are 4.89:1. `agent-browser a11y` (axe-core 4.12.1) reports color-contrast violations on `#test` (5 nodes), `#imported` (8), `#checks` (4) and `#check-fail` (3), and none on `#queue` (57 workload-tile nodes are marked "needs review"). The brief asks for AA.

## Method notes

- Verdicts rest on behaviour I observed. I read `app.js` (lock, route and sign code) only after seeing a behaviour, to explain it.
- Independent checks. The fixture hash came from `shasum -a 256`, queue counts and the assignment eligibility from `data.js` through Node, the balance errors from arithmetic, and contrast ratios from computed styles.
- The idle lock was observed by advancing the page's `Date.now` by script. No prototype file was touched.
- My headless session hung four times while three other verifiers shared the machine (a renderer at 100 % CPU and `agent-browser` answering "Resource temporarily unavailable"). The first hang came right after filling the flag Comment box. A step-for-step replay showed one 2 s stall while a DevTools watcher I had attached was also pausing the page. The third hang hit a fresh browser on `about:blank` before any prototype page had loaded. The fourth came after two checklist clicks that had run clean many times before, and after relaunching with `AGENT_BROWSER_NO_WEBMCP=1` the same steps ran clean. I attribute the hangs to the browser harness, not to the prototype, but I did not prove it. Each time I stopped only my own daemon and Chrome and restarted. Items 1 to 8 were driven in the earlier sessions and items 9 to 20 in the later ones, where I also re-ran the deep links, the upload, the assign dialog, the lock and switch cycles and the console reads.
- `data.js`, `gen-data.mjs` and the fixture were rewritten by someone else at 09:38 to 09:39 while I was testing. Compared with what I had recorded, only the record hash changed (`e94edde3...` to `78a1f6d0...`) and the limits (NEIPA 0.0828 to 1.25 ppm, and a new limits footnote citing the FDA CDER page, revision 30); the counts, eligibility reasons, checks, check-fail values and the import identity are the same. I re-ran the deep links, the upload, the flag and checklist gating, the signature prompt, the wrong-user, wrong-password and lockout cases, the signing and the `#sign` route against the new file, and refreshed those screenshots. Screenshots taken earlier and not refreshed (`06-*`, `12b-*`, `20-1366-test.png`, `20-1366-imported.png`, `20-1366-sign.png`, `20-reinjection-requested-sign-blocked.png`, `20-switch-user-carries-unsaved-work.png`, `20-manager-on-analyst-workspace.png`) still show the old hash `e94edde3` in the Record Version fields and the old NEIPA limit.
- The static server was restarted twice on the same port, once after the harness stopped it at its 30-minute background limit and once after I had stopped it. One transient file was written to `/tmp` and removed. Nothing else was written outside `verify/c2.md` and `shots/c2-verify/`, and the server and my browser session are stopped.
