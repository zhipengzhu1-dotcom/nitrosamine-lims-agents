## Anything else broken or misleading

### Serious

**1. Back out of the app and Forward reloads it, and the first-load rule then resets everything (the cause of partials 3, 5 and 6).**
The app changes routes with `replaceState`, so the only in-app history entries are the ones a typed hash creates, and the first Back usually leaves the page. Coming back with Forward reloads the app in this browser. A reload is a fresh first load (`RATIONALE.md`, "No persistence"), so it drops the lock and every lockout, turns signed Results back into drafts, and signs in the person named by the URL. Repro from a clean tab. Open `about:blank`, then `index.html#test`, lock the PC and fail five sign-ins for Priya at the lock screen (or sign the Test instead), press Back, press Forward. Priya was signed in with no lock and an empty lockout list (`06-j`, `06-k`). A signed Test came back as "Draft values, not yet Results", In Progress (`05-c`). Hannah on Checks today (URL `#checks`) came back as Kenji (`03-f`).
It reproduced only when the previous history entry was the blank page. With a real page before the app (`DECISION.md`), Back and then Forward restored the page from the back-forward cache with the lock and a JS marker intact, so a desktop Chrome with a normal previous page probably restores it. A cached page may also be dropped after a while, and the reload would then happen (an inference, not tested). I could not test a non-headless Chrome. A real build keeps the session, the lock, the lockouts and signatures on the server. For the prototype, keeping them in `sessionStorage` and ignoring the link's person when a session exists would close it.

**2. A second activation inside the close animation duplicates the action.**
The sign sheet and the assign sheet stay clickable and submittable for 180 and 170 ms while they slide out. Pressing Enter twice quickly in the sign form (two `press Enter` in one `agent-browser batch`) or two taps in one batch on "Sign failed Check as Performed" saved the failed BAL-04 Check twice. It opened DEV-26-0094 and DEV-26-0095, put two Equipment Deviation Holds on each of the 14 Tests (three on the three Tests that already had one), wrote two signature audit lines and rewrote BAL-04's Suspended reason to DEV-26-0095 (`20-a`, `20-b`). A passing BAL-05 Check submitted twice the same way wrote two signature audit lines and two save lines. A double tap on Assign wrote two "Assigned" rows and two audit entries. The Test signature is not affected, because its re-render clears the fields. The `dblclick` command did not reproduce it, and a human double tap lands on a button that is already sliding away, so the keyboard double press is the realistic trigger. Ignoring a second submit once the first has started (or disabling the button) fixes it.

## How I checked, and what I did not

- Clicks and typing went through the browser (`click`, `fill`, `keyboard type`, `press`, the on-screen keypad). A typed URL hash was `open <url>#route`, a same-document fragment navigation, which is the event the address bar produces. Back and Forward were `back` and `forward`.
- Simulated. The idle lock was reached by setting `App.S.lastActivity` 16 minutes back. I read `App.S` and `App.debug.signingContent()` only to prove what a screen showed. Expected values (the 14 Test IDs, the counts 38, 49, 9, 23, 33 and 45, the credentials) came from `data.js` through Node, and the record hash was recomputed with my own canonical JSON and `crypto.subtle`.
- One of my scripted loops never ran its typed-hash steps because of zsh word splitting. I discarded its output and its screenshot and reran it with quoted commands.
- Not tested. A real touchscreen or gloves, a screen reader, a non-headless Chrome, Edge or Windows, a real 15-minute wait, and the freezer excursion, DI water and pipette entry (not among the ten items).
- My server and both browser sessions (`verify-chosen`, and the earlier probe) are stopped.
