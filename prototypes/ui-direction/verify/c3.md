## Item 20: broken, misleading or confusing

### Serious

1. **Any URL hash change signs in the other persona with no credentials.** Signing in as Priya with credentials and then pressing the browser Back button made Hannah the signed-in Lab Manager, with no credentials, no "Who is using this PC now?" screen and no toast (`20-back-button-silent-user-change.png`, which also shows the earlier "Signed in as Priya Raman" toast still up while the plate reads Hannah). Forward and a hand-typed hash do the same. Starting as Kenji (Analyst) on `#checks` and changing only the hash to `#assign` gave a Lab Manager session, and completing an assignment there recorded "hkowalski assigned T26-04176 to abello" in the Audit Trail (`20-analyst-becomes-manager-via-hash.png`). The blocking "Who is using this PC now?" screen is dismissed the same way (`20-switch-screen-bypassed-by-hash.png`). The lock screen is not affected. This is the hidden role toggle the brief says to avoid, and it defeats the shared-PC promise that a change of user needs credentials. Likely cause, read from `js/app.js` lines 35 and 60 and not observed: the `hashchange` handler calls `apply()`, which starts a session for the route's persona whenever it differs from the signed-in person.

### Moderate

2. **The FRZ-01 consequences are hard to see.** Also the reason for item 17. At 1366x768 the whole "If you save this, the LIMS will" block is below the fold while the red "Save the failing reading" button is in view (`20-1366-freezer-consequences-below-fold.png`), and at 1920x1080 "Refuses new placements into FRZ-01" is hidden behind the footer until the sheet is scrolled (`17-freezer-1080-unscrolled-consequences.png`). There is no scroll hint. The BAL-04 sheet fits on both sizes (`20-1366-check-fail.png`).
3. **"Account locked" says the PC is locked, but it is not.** The dialog says "Until then this PC is locked to Priya; someone else can take it over" (`15-account-locked.png`), yet after closing it the plate still read "Signed in as Priya Raman", the workspace was open and the Sign Performed button was still offered, and only signing and sign-in were refused.
4. **Acknowledging the alarm hides the gap.** "Acknowledge the alarm" is one tap with no reason, and it removes both the banner and the "Missed yesterday: no reading on 2026-09-29" line on the RD-102 card, although no reading exists for that day (`20-alarm-acknowledge.png`).
5. **The first queue tile is unreliable.** With Overdue on it reads "19 All open" (`02-queue-overdue-19.png`). With Closed on it reads "327 All shown" beside a label of "1276 Tests shown", and "Closed" adds the closed Tests to the table rather than showing only closed ones (`20-queue-closed-toggle.png`).

### Minor

6. **"Ready since" changes after assigning.** It moves from 08:41 to the assignment time (10:44 EDT) although the Test is no longer Ready (`05-assign-committed.png`).
7. **A toast covers the "Back to the queue" button.** After assigning, the confirmation toast sits over that button for about 4 seconds (`05-assign-committed.png`).
8. **"Open" means two things.** The workload chips count Assigned plus In Progress, but tapping a chip lists every open Test the person has: Kenji's chip says "9 open" and tapping it shows 15 Tests, and after the assignment Priya's chip said "7 open" while the Assignee filter (labelled "Priya Raman (14)") listed 14 Tests (2 Assigned, 5 In Progress, 4 Submitted for Review, 3 Reviewed).
9. **The take-over screen has no "someone else" path.** It lists only Hannah, Priya and Kenji, and says "Someone else? Only people with an account here can sign in" with no control to do that, so a colleague such as Daniel Cho (who Verified the Preparations) cannot sign in (`06-nav-to-test-as-manager.png`).
10. **ARIA errors.** The assignment radios and the two checklist checkboxes start with an empty `aria-checked=""`, which axe reports as critical `aria-required-attr` (10 nodes on `#assign`, 2 on `#imported`), and with any sheet open axe also reports a duplicate banner landmark (moderate).
11. **The page title goes stale.** It reads "Hannah Kowalski: Lab queue" while the assignment panel is open and "Priya Raman: Sign Performed" after that prompt is closed.
12. **A name is cut off.** "Kenji Watanabe ..." is truncated in the assignment list while other names with native names show in full (`04-assign-panel-13-and-7.png`).
13. **Ready-made comment phrases.** The flag dialog offers chips that insert sentences such as "Checked the peak in MassLynx: the integration is correct", so a flag can be accepted without the Analyst having looked (`11-flag-dialog.png`). This is a design risk, not a defect.

### Checked and found fine

- No horizontal overflow and no interactive target under 40 px on any of the seven routes at 1366x768 (measured; `20-1366-queue.png`, `20-1366-sign.png`, `20-1366-imported.png` and the others).
- WCAG AA text contrast: 0 failing text elements on the seven routes at 1920x1080, computed from rendered colors (4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text). Axe found 0 violations on `#queue`, `#test` and `#checks`.
- Every queue filter I tried shows as many rows as its label says (Method, Customer, GxP Class, Holds, Assignee, At risk 47), and the counts I could recompute from `data.js` match (open 327, Ready 28, Overdue 19, GMP 298 and non-GMP 29, With Holds 38, method M01 139, customer C01 30, unassigned 105). I could not recompute "At risk" from the data. Column sort works in both directions.
- Screens agree with each other: after assigning, signing T26-04175 and failing BAL-04, the Lab queue showed the new state (Equipment Deviation holds 9 to 23, 14 rows linked to DEV-26-0094; `20-queue-after-bal04-fail-holds.png`, `20-queue-after-signing-T26-04175.png`).
- Deviation numbers are distinct within one session (DEV-26-0094, then DEV-26-0095).
- An implausible value (99 C in a room) gets its own wording and the same second-entry gate (`20-implausible-value.png`), and the failing BAL-04 state is also reachable from `#checks` by typing the weighings (`20-bal04-clickthrough-typed.png`).
- Escape cancels the signature prompt, focus starts in the User ID field, and the simulated passkey signs after about 1.3 seconds.
- Glossary avoid-words: no hits beyond words the brief itself uses ("draft values", "missed reading", "alarm"), and "Room Deviation" is a valid Deviation Kind.

## Notes on method

- Instrument check: `agent-browser console` and `errors` caught a deliberately injected error and exception before I relied on them. `errors --clear` does not clear, so I relaunched the session and read `errors --json` from then on.
- One script misjudged the FRZ-01 footer position at 1920x1080. The screenshot (`17-freezer-1080-unscrolled-consequences.png`) is the evidence for item 17, not the script.
- The harness stopped my first server at its 30 minute background limit. I restarted it on the same port and stopped it when done.
- I did not edit the prototype, did not run `verify/flows.mjs`, and did not use the Chrome extension browser.
