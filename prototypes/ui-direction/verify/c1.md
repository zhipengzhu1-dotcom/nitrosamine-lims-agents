Checked on 2026-09-30 by driving `candidates/c1/index.html` with agent-browser 0.37.1 (headless Chrome 153, own session `verify-c1`). Every checklist flow works as specified and the console stayed clean. The prototype still has three defects a lab user could be hurt by, all under item 20. An unsaved draft reading leaks to the next person who takes over the PC and is saved under their name. A Lab Manager can sign a Check as Performed and the signature records her role as Analyst. ## Item 20: broken, misleading or confusing

### Serious

**1. An unsaved draft leaks across a takeover and is saved under the next person's name.**
Signed in as Priya on Checks, tap 21.5 into RD-102 Temperature and leave it unsaved. Press Switch user, choose Someone else, and type kwatanabe, bench-demo-2026 and 271828. The lock screen had promised "Signing in ends Priya Raman's session. Anything they had not saved stays as their draft." Kenji's RD-102 form opens with Temperature 21.5 °C, "Within limits". He types only Humidity 45 and presses Save reading. The record reads "Saved by Kenji Watanabe at 10:42 EDT (14:42 UTC), server time … Temperature 21.5 °C … Humidity 45 % RH". Unlocking as the same person correctly keeps the draft, so drafts are not scoped to a person. Screenshots: `07-takeover-someone-else.png`, `07-takeover-result-kenji.png`, `07-draft-leak-saved-under-kenji.png`.

**2. A Lab Manager can sign a balance Check as Performed, and the signature says her role is Analyst.**
In data.js Hannah Kowalski has roles `["Lab Manager"]` and no authorisations. Signed in as her, the Checks today tab is fully usable (the Test tab, by contrast, says "Read only for you"). I entered passing BAL-05 weighings and pressed Sign Performed. The prompt said "hkowalski, signing as Analyst, Lab RD". After signing with hkowalski, bench-demo-2026 and 161803 the block read "Signed by Hannah Kowalski, Username hkowalski, Role Analyst", while the rail plate beside it said Lab Manager. Screenshots: `20-lab-manager-can-sign-performed.png`, `20-lab-manager-signature-role-analyst.png`.

**3. Failed-Check consequences are announced but never applied elsewhere.**
After BAL-04 failed, the screen said "Holds placed on the 14 Tests that cited BAL-04" and "DEV-26-0094 opened" (0095 in `18-bal04-signed-suspended.png`, which followed the freezer failure). In the Lab queue, filtered to Equipment Deviation Holds, the same 9 Tests appear as before, each linked to DEV-26-0091 (one also to the older DEV-26-0085) and none to the new Deviation. The Holds filter still reads "With open Holds (38)" and "Equipment Deviation (9)", and the Deviation filter lists no new Deviation. The new number appears only in the screen-reader live region. The freezer Excursion behaves the same, and both Suspended statuses show only on the Checks screen. The brief only asked the screen to say what saving will do, so item 18 passes, but a Lab Manager reading the queue after a failed balance Check would see no Holds. Screenshots: `18-bal04-signed-suspended.png`, `20-failed-check-not-reflected-in-queue.png`.

### Moderate

**4. Changing the URL hash silently changes who is signed in.**
With Hannah signed in on #queue, setting the address-bar hash to #checks made Kenji Watanabe the signed-in person with no credentials, no notice and all unsaved work reset. The tabs do not do this, only a hash change does. It is the prototype's deep-link contract, but a person can trigger it, and it undercuts "who is signed in must be obvious". Screenshot: `20-hash-change-swaps-person.png`.

**5. The Record Version 2 hash does not depend on the comment the Analyst types.**
Accepting the flag with my own comment gave e94edde3, the same hash the #sign deep link shows for its seeded comment. Requesting a Reinjection instead gave a different, computed hash (39b2fb42). The prompt says the signature binds "the SHA-256 of this version", so on the accept path the shown hash is not a hash of what was typed. Screenshots: `11-flag-accepted-checklist-open.png`, `01-deeplink-sign.png`, `20-reinjection-path-state.png`.

**6. Keyboard and screen-reader gaps.**
- axe (WCAG 2 A and AA) found one serious violation on #imported and #sign, `scrollable-region-focusable` on `#ws-doc`. That region holds 1,649 px of content below the fold (Run checks, Injections, Preparations) and cannot be scrolled by keyboard. It found no violations on the other five routes and no colour-contrast violations, though it could not evaluate one contrast node in the assignment panel.
- `aria-label` sits on generic elements (62 nodes on #imported, for example `aria-label="no peak"` on a cell that shows only a dash), which some screen readers ignore.
- Reaching the first queue row by keyboard takes 45 Tab presses and there is no skip link. The `/` shortcut, arrow keys and Enter do work.
- After Escape or Cancel closes the sign prompt, focus drops to the page body instead of the button that opened it.

### Minor

7. **Stale and short-lived messages.** After ticking the last checklist item the rail still showed "Not yet. Tick the 2 remaining checklist items first." beside the now-enabled "Sign Performed…" button. The assignment confirmation ("Recorded in the audit trail… Audited, not signed.") disappeared from the rail after about 9 seconds, and only the row's state history keeps the record afterwards.
8. **Glossary drift.** The Import button and steps say "Upload TargetLynx export" and "Uploaded 2 files", the card is headed "Imported file" (Import avoids "Upload (alone), file"), and the Injections table has a "Prep" column (Preparation avoids "prep"). I scanned the rendered text of every state for the glossary's Avoid terms and found no other real drift.
9. **A workload tile and its list disagree without explanation.** The tile reads "Daniel Cho: 14 active, 11 In Progress", and clicking it lists 18 Tests (3 Submitted for Review and 1 Reviewed are added).
10. **A 2-place balance accepts four decimals.** BAL-05 took 100.0002 g. The saved reading is displayed as "100.00 g" while its error (+0.0001 %) was computed from the unrounded digits.
11. **Long names and values clip.** "Kenji Watanabe 渡辺 健二" breaks mid-name on the lock tiles and is cut off ("渡辺 …") in the assignment list, and the closed Holds select shows "Equipment De…" so the active filter cannot be read.
12. **Demo aids that a product must not have.** The sign prompt and lock screen print the password and the current authenticator code, and "Fill demo credentials" fills the User ID as well (observed on #sign), which item 12 says must be typed.
13. **Only T26-04175 can be opened.** "Open workspace" appears only for that row (T26-04019 offered just "Assign…"), and the Test tab is fixed to T26-04175 for everyone. This looks like prototype scope.
14. **Data oddity, not the prototype's fault.** T26-04120 shows Ready since 21 Sep 10:25 (all three history rows in the same minute) while its Sample was received on 29 Sep.

## How I checked, and what I did not

- Clicks were real pointer events through the browser and text was typed with fill or the on-screen keypad. The exceptions are stated here. The queue timing test called click() from script, the page clock was advanced for the idle lock, the URL hash was set for finding 4, and the workspace scroll position was set for one screenshot.
- Expected values came from `data.js`, and the arithmetic and the fixture hash were recomputed independently.
- Not tested: a real touchscreen or gloves (I measured target sizes instead), a screen reader, Windows or Edge, and a real 15-minute wait.
- My first server hit the 30-minute background limit and was stopped by the harness during the run. I restarted it with a longer limit for a few final checks and stopped it again at the end.
- Files outside c1 changed on disk while I worked, and I did not touch them. The harness `index.html` changed at 08:42, `gen-data.mjs` at 08:35 and `todo.md` was removed. `data.js` and all of c1 are unchanged, so the values above are against the 02:24 `data.js`.
