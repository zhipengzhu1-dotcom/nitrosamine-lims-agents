# c4 (Ledger & Rail) click-through verification

Checked by driving `candidates/c4/index.html` in headless Chrome with `agent-browser` 0.37.1 (session `verify-c4`), served by `python3 -m http.server 8784` from `prototypes/ui-direction`. The prototype was not edited. Primary viewport 1920×1080, layout also checked at 1366×768. Screenshots are in `shots/c4-verify/`. Times quoted are the app's clock, which starts at 10:40 EDT on every load.

How the labels were decided. **pass** means I saw the item work on the path it describes and found no way past it. **partial** means it works on that path but I found a reproducible way past it, or a gap. **fail** means it does not hold on the path the item describes, or (item 20) I found a break a lab user can reach. Sections were reloaded between each other as the checklist asks. E continued from D.

Where a number could be recomputed independently I did that from `data.js` or the fixture file, so the evidence is not the prototype's own claim. Every instrument I relied on got a positive control first (a deliberate console error, an uncaught exception, a low-contrast probe, a selection that must register).

Shared files changed under me. `data.js`, `gen-data.mjs` and the fixture were rewritten by someone else at 09:38 to 09:39 while I was testing (c4's own files were last modified at 03:36 and were never touched). After noticing, I re-ran every number and string this report quotes against fresh loads under the new data: the fixture hash is unchanged, the counts, the 13/7 and 6/14 eligibility, the 20 workload figures, the prompts, limits and hashes all still match, so findings from before and after the change are comparable.

## Console and page errors

None from the prototype. I read the console buffer after each group of flows and again at the very end, and it held only my own deliberate probe lines (`PROBE-console-error`, `PROBE-console-warn`, `PROBE2-before-navigation`, `PROBE2-warn`), each cleared after being read; the final read showed 0 messages. The page-error buffer holds one entry for the whole session, my own `PROBE-uncaught`. Network: the seven prototype files were fetched 77 times each, all 200, plus Google Fonts (242 requests, all 200); `fixtures/…CompoundSummary.txt` was fetched 7 times, which is how the "recomputed, matches" chip is produced.

Instrument notes. `agent-browser errors` prints a bare `✗` when the buffer holds an entry and `--clear` did not empty it, so I read `errors --json` and treated my one probe as the baseline. Axe 4.12.1 (WCAG 2.0 to 2.2 A and AA) gave 0 violations on all seven states. Its "incomplete" items are contrast it cannot compute over hatched or gradient areas and avatar initials, plus one `aria-label` on a plain `div` (`.status-counts` on `#checks`). I therefore computed contrast myself for every visible text node on six screens (positive control: injected `#aaa` on white was flagged at 2.32), with none below AA.

## Item 20: broken, misleading or confusing

### A. Hash routing is treated as authentication (items 7, 10, 15)

Changing the URL hash in a live session acts as a completed sign-in for that route's person and re-seeds the session. The designer lists "Deep links skip authentication" as a known demo affordance. What I observed goes further than that entry says.

1. **Lock bypass.** With the PC locked (idle lock, 10:55), setting the hash to `#queue-x` cleared the lock and returned Hannah's work with no credentials; setting `#checks` then signed Kenji in. Evidence `07g-lock-bypassed-by-hash.png`.
2. **Account lockout bypass.** After five wrong attempts the "ACCOUNT LOCKED" card disappeared on a hash edit to `#test`. The switch screen still labelled Priya "account locked", yet at `#sign` her correct credentials signed the Test ("Signed Performed · T26-04175 is Submitted for Review"). Evidence `15f-account-lock-cleared-by-hash.png`, `15h-locked-account-sign-attempt.png`.
3. **Signature gate bypass.** From a fresh `#test` (nothing imported), `#sign` in the address bar opens a prompt that says the flag was "accepted with comment" and the checklist "3 of 3 confirmed", and signing succeeds ("The drafts are now your Results"). Evidence `10e-sign-prompt-without-any-import.png`, `10f-signed-without-import-or-flag.png`. The seeded flag comment is stamped 10:31 EDT, before a 10:40 upload in the same session (`12x-cancel-signs-nothing.png`).
4. **Any person, no credentials.** Hash `#test`, `#checks` or `#queue` signs that person in with only a toast (about 9 s) to say so ("Priya Raman signed in on this PC · Kenji Watanabe's session ended"). Evidence `06g-kenji-hash-to-test.png`.

### B. Back then Upload un-signs a Test (no URL editing)

Sequence from a clean load: `#test`, Upload and parse, Accept with comment (typed), None, Confirm, sign as `praman`. The Test is Submitted for Review with a signature block. Press Back three times. The page is `#test` showing "Import the TargetLynx export" and an Upload button while the header still says "Submitted for Review". Upload and parse again. The signed Results are replaced by draft values, the signature block is gone, and "Sign Performed…" returns, still under "Submitted for Review". Evidence `20-back-to-test-after-signed.png`, `20-reupload-after-signed.png`. Two related variants. Setting the hash to `#test` after signing shows the same mismatch (`10d-hash-test-after-signed.png`). Back after the first import shows the pre-import view although the import already exists (`20-back-after-upload.png`). By contrast, `#sign` typed after signing is refused correctly and the page stays on `#imported` (`10c-hash-sign-after-signed.png`).

### C. Gaps against the brief

- **DI water cannot be entered.** DIW-01 is the only DI water tile, it is already Done, and it opens read-only ("18.2 MΩ·cm · 4 ppb"), so resistivity and TOC entry can never be reached. The brief lists it as an entry that must work.
- **pH and pipette Checks are stubs.** PH-01, PIP-07 and PIP-11 open a read-only sheet saying "Entering pH and pipette Checks is not part of this prototype." That is honest, but PIP-07 and PIP-11 are Due soon and Overdue.
- **Announced consequences do not travel.** After the failed BAL-04 Check ("Holds placed on 14 Tests") the Lab queue still shows "38 held" and "Deviations 33" (`18f-queue-after-failed-check.png`). The designer lists this one. After acknowledging the missed-reading alarm the banner turns into a green audit line, but the RD-102 tile still reads "Missed yesterday · alarm open" (`20-alarm-acknowledged.png`).

### D. Confusing or misleading, smaller

- **14 versus 18.** Tapping "Daniel Cho: 14 open" in the workload strip lists 18 rows: 3 Assigned, 11 In Progress, 3 Submitted for Review, 1 Reviewed. Nothing on screen explains the extra four (`20-queue-analyst-filter-18-vs-14.png`).
- **Two tiles both titled CMB-01** (freezer and fridge compartments); only the subtitle tells them apart (`01-deeplink-checks.png`).
- **The re-type prompt prints the value to re-type** ("Type the 200 g reading 200.1834 g again to confirm", "Type −60 °C again to confirm"), so double entry can be done without looking at the display (`18b-retype-mismatch.png`).
- **A tick on an unselected choice.** In "Min/max memory", "Reset after reading" carries a ✓ icon while "Not reset" is the selected (dark) option (`17d-frz01-ready-to-save-out-of-limit.png`).
- **Stale clock text.** Reading sheets say "now 10:41 EDT" and never update, while the top bar had moved to 10:44 (`17d-frz01-ready-to-save-out-of-limit.png`).
- **Checks appear only after Next.** The error for the last balance weighing and the limit check for a typed reading show "Press Next to compute/check" until Next or blur, and the Save button can already be enabled (`20-bal05-passing-typed.png`). A failing value kept Save disabled, so no failing value was saved without its warning.
- **Audit-trail confirmations are toasts** that last about 9 s. The permanent record (Details › History) does not say "audited, not signed".
- **Truncation.** At 1366×768 hold reasons ("Equipment Deviati…"), customer names and tile text are cut with ellipses (`20-1366-queue.png`, `20-1366-checks.png`). At 1920×1080 the FRZ-01 tile after an excursion repeats itself and is cut: "new placements refused · refuses…" (`17e-frz01-saved-excursion.png`).
- **The Test's hash ignores what the Analyst typed.** Record Version 2 shows `e94edde3…` whatever flag comment is entered, while the Check hashes are computed and change with the readings (2000.40 g and 2000.41 g gave different hashes). The designer lists this one.
- **BAL-05 lists a 2 kg check weight** under "Check weights CW-01 OIML E2 (1 mg–200 g)". The data has no 2 kg set (`20-bal05-sheet-empty.png`).
- **Demo scaffolding on real controls.** Passwords and codes are printed on prompts and lock screens ("Demo only"), and the locked-account card offers "Reset the demo" beside "Someone else signs in" (not clicked).
- **Keyboard.** The first table row is the 42nd Tab stop on the queue, and it is the only row in the tab order (arrow keys move between rows, Enter and Space open Details while a click only selects). Every stop showed a visible focus indicator: a 3 px blue outline, or a shadow ring on the search box.
- **Target height.** The queue's filter controls and the disabled "Use a passkey" button are 40 px tall. Every other control I measured on the bench screens is 47 px or more, and on sheets and the lock screen 56 px.

### Checked and fine

No horizontal overflow, the rail visible and no control under 40 px on all seven deep links at 1366×768. No `alert`, `confirm`, `prompt` or `window.open` in the source. "Fictional data" chip on every screen including the lock screens. Filters match the data (GMP 298, non-GMP 29, Holds 38, Deviations 33, Unassigned 105, Method CO-MTH-0018 v1 38, Larchmont 31, method plus customer 0 with an empty-state message), the At risk chip's 32 equals its rows (not recomputed from the data), and all four sorts work. The workload strip matches the data for all 20 Analysts. Assignment eligibility for T26-04120 (6 eligible, 14 refused) equals my independent recomputation from Training Records and Authorisations, after the same rule reproduced the server's answer for T26-04176 for all 20 Analysts. A passing BAL-05 verification signs end to end, and Deviation IDs increment within a session (DEV-26-0094 for FRZ-01, DEV-26-0095 for BAL-04).

## Not verified

Real touchscreen and glove use, screen readers beyond axe, the PDF's own hash (no PDF fixture exists in the repo), pH and pipette entry (stubs), and DI water entry (unreachable). Persistence was also not testable: a reload resets the attempt counter, locks and all work (observed twice for the five-attempt lockout), which the designer lists as "No persistence".
