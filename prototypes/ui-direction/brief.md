# Brief: the UI direction for a nitrosamine LC-MS/MS LIMS

You are one of several designers answering the same question independently. Commit to one clear direction for how this app should look and feel at the bench, and build it as a working prototype. The owner will flip between the directions and pick one or graft parts together, so a strong point of view beats a safe average.

## The question

What should the app look and feel like for daily bench use: dense data entry on shared lab PCs, glove-friendly targets, clear status, and signature prompts? It must hold up at this lab's real volume.

## Who uses it, and where

- **The lab.** A pharmaceutical R&D lab (Lab code `RD`) that tests drug substances (APIs) for nitrosamine impurities, mostly by LC-MS/MS, under 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11 and ISO/IEC 17025. GMP and non-GMP work share one lab.
- **Volume.** 500+ Samples a month, about 300 open Tests at any time, 20 Analysts, 3 Lab Managers, 2 QA, 2 Sample Custodians.
- **At the bench.** Shared all-in-one PCs with touchscreens, running Windows and Chrome or Edge, mostly 1920×1080 and some 1366×768. Analysts often stand and wear nitrile gloves. Several people use the same PC in a shift. Lighting is bright and even.
- **At a desk.** Lab Managers, Reviewers and QA work with mouse and keyboard on ordinary monitors, Windows or macOS.
- The stack for the real build will be React 19 + TypeScript. The prototype does not need to be React.

## The domain rules the screens must respect

Use the glossary's terms exactly: `/Users/maracchi/Desktop/Claude/9. September 2026/29-Sep-2026 Tests/09-29-2026 Test/CONTEXT.md` (read it; it is the domain language). When the UI names one of its concepts, use the glossary term, never a word the glossary lists under _Avoid_ for that concept.

- **Test states:** Requested → Accepted | Rejected → Ready → Assigned → In Progress → Submitted for Review → Reviewed → Reported; side exits Cancelled and Invalidated. A **Hold** is a separate record that blocks named steps without changing the state; several can be open on one Test.
- **GxP Class:** GMP or non-GMP, on every Test.
- **Fitness Status** of equipment, Packs and Solutions: Quarantined, In use, Suspended, Expired, Retired. The screen always states the reason, for example "Calibration expired since 2026-09-12".
- **Deviation:** Kind, Risk Level (Minor, Major, Critical) and state (Open, Investigating, In QA Review, Closed).
- **Electronic Signature.** Every signing re-authenticates in full: the person types their user ID and password, then gives a fresh second factor, either a TOTP code or a passkey. The login session never counts. Each signature carries exactly one **Signature Meaning** (Performed, Verified, Reviewed, Approved, Released, Authored, Acknowledged) with a fixed statement, and binds to one **Record Version** by the SHA-256 hash of its content. Five consecutive failures, at login or signing, lock the account. Once signed, a record shows a signature block: printed name, username, role, meaning, UTC and lab-local time with the zone, the record version and the first 8 characters of its hash.
- **Assignment** of a Test is refused, with no override, unless the Analyst has a Training Record on the Method version, Training Records on its prerequisite Documents, and a current Performed Authorisation for the Method in this Lab. The Lab Manager assigns; it is audited, not signed.
- **Imported values are drafts.** A worker parses the uploaded TargetLynx TXT (with the PDF as a cross-checked attachment) into draft values. They become the Analyst's results only when the Analyst confirms them by signing Performed. Each flag on a draft must be handled first.
- **Readings** (rooms, freezers, fridges, DI water) are audited but unsigned; balance and other verification Checks are signed Performed. A reading's time is server time; nobody types a time. A value outside its acceptance limits, or outside its plausibility range, must be typed a second time to confirm before it saves. Before saving a failing value the screen says what will happen: a failed balance Check opens an Equipment Deviation, suspends the balance and puts Holds on every Test that cited it since its last passing Check; a storage unit out of limits opens an Excursion Deviation and refuses new placements.
- **Shared PCs.** Who is signed in must be obvious at all times. Idle sessions lock after 15 minutes. Someone else must be able to take over the PC quickly and safely.
- Everything is fictional. Show a small "Fictional data" marker somewhere on every screen. Limits are illustrative.

## What to build: four screens, seven deep links

Build one working web prototype. Every deep link below must render its state directly on a fresh load, and every state must also be reachable by clicking through. Routes are hash-based and must be exactly these:

1. `#queue`: **Lab queue**, signed in as the Lab Manager (`personas.queue`). All open Tests, usable at the full volume. Per Test: Test ID, Sample, Customer, Product and Lot, Method (number, version, short title), GxP Class, service level, state and how long it has been in it, due date with overdue and at-risk clearly marked, assignee or unassigned, open Holds (count, reason on demand) and linked Deviations. The Lab Manager must be able to filter by state, assignee (including unassigned), Method, Customer, GxP Class, Holds and overdue; search any ID; see the counts per state; and see the workload of all 20 Analysts.
2. `#assign`: the queue with the **assignment** of `focus.assignTestId` open. All 20 Analysts appear. Refused ones cannot be chosen and say why; eligible ones show their open workload. Assigning moves the Test to Assigned and says it was recorded in the audit trail.
3. `#test`: the **Test workspace** at the bench, signed in as the Analyst (`personas.test`), before the import. It shows the Test, its Preparations (weight, volume, balance, pipettes, DI water, entered by, Verified by), the Run with its instrument, column and Solutions and their Fitness Status, and the Notebook Entry reference. The Analyst can upload the TargetLynx export (simulate the upload; the data already holds the parse).
4. `#imported`: the same workspace after the import. It shows the file's identity (name, hash, dataset path, parser, PDF cross-check), the draft values for this Test's injections, each Preparation's result, the Reportable Result (mean of Preparations) against LOQ and the illustrative limit, the run checks, and the flag on NDMA. The Analyst must handle the flag and the Performed checklist before signing. Dense data, read and acted on at the bench.
5. `#sign`: the **signature prompt** for signing Performed on the focus Test. It makes unmistakable who is signing, the meaning and its statement, and exactly what is being signed (record, Record Version, hash). The typed user ID must match the signed-in person. Wrong credentials show an error and the attempts left before lockout; the demo credentials succeed. After signing, the signature block appears on the record and the Test moves to Submitted for Review. Cancelling is always possible, and nothing gets signed by accident.
6. `#checks`: **today's Checks** at the bench, signed in as the Analyst (`personas.checks`). Every item in `checksToday` with its status (Done, Due, Due soon, Overdue, Blocked, Not used today), and the missed reading alarm. Entering a reading or a balance verification must work with gloves on a touchscreen: rooms (temperature and humidity; min and max for the storage room), storage units (current, min and max since reset, reset), DI water (resistivity and TOC), balances (the raw weighings, with the error and pass or fail computed). Balance verification signs Performed with the same signature prompt.
7. `#check-fail`: the balance verification for BAL-04 with the failing weighings from `focus.checkFail` typed in, showing the failure, what saving it will do, and the confirmation by typing the value again.

The screens are one app: navigation between them should feel like the real product (the signed-in person changes with the persona; show that as a switch of user on the shared PC, not as a hidden role toggle).

## Technical constraints

- Static files only, no build step. Your entry is `index.html` in your own directory; you may add `.css` and `.js` files beside it. Vanilla JS, or a library from `cdnjs.cloudflare.com`, `cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/` or `unpkg.com`. Fonts only from Google Fonts or the system. Nothing else external.
- Never call `alert`, `confirm`, `prompt` or `window.open`. They block the test harness.
- It must work inside an iframe and on its own, from a plain static server. Set your own page background; do not depend on the OS light or dark setting.
- No horizontal page scrolling at 1366×768. Keep the console free of errors.
- `localStorage` is optional; wrap any use in `try`/`catch`.
- Keyboard reachable, visible focus, labelled inputs, WCAG AA contrast. Color is never the only signal.
- Do not read or copy anything under `OneDrive_3_9-29-2026/` or any other candidate's directory.

## Checking your work

From the `ui-direction` directory, `node shoot.mjs candidates/<you>` serves the folder, loads each deep link in headless Chrome at 1920×1080 and 1366×768, saves screenshots to `shots/<you>/`, and prints console errors, horizontal overflow and a count of interactive targets under 40 px. Look at the screenshots with the Read tool and fix what is wrong. Do not use the Chrome extension browser; it is the owner's.
